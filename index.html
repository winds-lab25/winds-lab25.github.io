About the lab

We build real solutions to real networking problems, then measure whether they actually hold up.

Based in the Department of Mathematics and Computing at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, WiNDS Lab improves the performance, scalability, and efficiency of IoT networks and distributed systems.

We work close to the metal, with protocols, simulators, and testbeds, and pair that with AI/ML to push what resource-constrained networks can do.

  1. Step 01

    Simulate

    Reproducible simulators such as 6TiSCH-Sim explore protocol behaviour at scale, before touching hardware.

  2. Step 02

    Test on real hardware

    Our IoT testbed grounds every result in real radios, real interference, and real energy budgets.

  3. Step 03

    Learn and adapt

    Deep RL and ML turn measurements into controllers that adapt scheduling, routing, and control.
